# S. 2058

A bill to suspend temporarily the duty on knit garments of stretch fabric with textile or polymer-based electrodes knit into or attached to the fabric ant that incorporate connectors designed to secure an electronic transmitter that transmits physiological information from the electrodes to a compatible monitor.
