- 2009-03-05 — DEBATE - Pursuant to the provisions of H. Res. 190, the Committee of the Whole proceeded with thirty minutes of debate on the Lofgren amendment as modified. The modification would (1) allow a court to consider, in lieu of reducing mortgage principal, lowering the interest rate to reduce the debtor's mortgage payments according to the Administration's Homeowner Affordability and Stability Plan as implemented on March 4, 2009; (2) give the mortgage holder a greater proportion of the appreciation in a home on a graduated basis if it is sold during the 5-year period that the case is pending; and (3) provide that when a debtor seeks to reduce a mortgage's principal, the court must determine whether such relief is in good faith based on whether debtor was offered a mortgage modification under the Administration's plan and the debtor was able to afford payments under such plan.
