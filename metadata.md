# H.Res. 218

Waiving a requirement of clause 6(a) of rule XIII with respect to consideration of certain resolutions reported from the Committee on Rules.

> Recorded as of this version. Later cosponsors and actions are
> omitted, so this file is the state of the measure at this point in
> its progress, not its final record.

## Sponsor

- Rep. McGovern, James P. [D-MA-3] (M000312)

## Actions

- 2009-03-05 — Introduced in House
- 2009-03-05 — Placed on the House Calendar, Calendar No. 15.
- 2009-03-05 — The House Committee on Rules reported an original measure, H. Rept. 111-24, by Mr. McGovern.
- 2009-03-05 — The House Committee on Rules reported an original measure, H. Rept. 111-24, by Mr. McGovern.
- 2009-03-05 — The resolution waives clause 6(a) of rule XIII (requiring a two-thirds vote to consider a rule on the same day it is reported from the Rules Committee) against certain resolutions reported from the Rules Committee. The waiver is applied to any resolution reported on the legislative day of March 6, 2009, providing for consideration or disposition of any measure making appropriations for the fiscal year 2009, and for other purposes.
