# S. 390

> To expand the authority of the Secretary of the Air Force to convey certain relocatable military housing units to Indian tribes located in Idaho and Nevada.

## § 1. Expanded authority to convey relocatable military housing units

Section 8032 of the Department of Defense Appropriations Act, 2009 (division C of Public Law 110–329; 122 Stat. 3628) is amended—

- **(1)** by inserting “Idaho, Nevada,” after “States of” both places it appears; and
- **(2)** in subsection (a), by striking “and Minot Air Force Base” and inserting “, Minot Air Force Base, Mountain Home Air Force Base, and Malmstrom Air Force Base”.
