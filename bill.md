# S. 2438

> To modify and extend the temporary suspension of duty on certain vacuum relief valves designed for use in aircraft.

## § 1. Certain vacuum relief valves designed for use in aircraft

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.22.83 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to vacuum relief valves) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking the article description and inserting the following: “Vacuum relief valves for equalizing pressure between the internal cabin and the external atmosphere certified by the importer for use in civil aircraft (provided for in subheading 8481.40.00)”; and
  - **(2)** by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) apply with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
