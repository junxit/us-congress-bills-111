# H.R. 1514

> To amend the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 to reauthorize the juvenile accountability block grants program through fiscal year 2014.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Juvenile Accountability Block Grants Program Reauthorization Act of 2010”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of Juvenile Accountability Block Grants program through fiscal year 2014

Part R of title I of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (42 U.S.C. 3796ee et seq.) is amended—

- **(1)** in section 1801A(a), by striking “section 1810(b)” and inserting “section 1810(c)”;
- **(2)** in section 1810(a), by striking “2009” and inserting “2014”; and
- **(3)** in section 1810(b), by inserting “and each of the fiscal years 2009 through 2014” after “2004”.
