# H. RES. 51

> Electing Members to certain standing committees of the House of Representatives.

That the following named Members be and are hereby elected to the following standing committees of the House of Representatives:

- **(1)** *Committee on House Administration* Ms. Zoe Lofgren of California, Mr. Capuano, Mr. Gonzalez, Mrs. Davis of California, Mr. Davis of Alabama.
- **(2)** *Committee on Rules* Mr. Perlmutter, Ms. Pingree of Maine, Mr. Polis of Colorado.

## § 2.

Paragraph (5) of House Resolution 24, One Hundred Eleventh Congress, agreed to January 7, 2009, is amended by striking “Mr. Bishop of Utah,” and inserting “Mr. Bishop of New York,”.
