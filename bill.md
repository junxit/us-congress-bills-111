# S. 2981

> To reevaluate and redirect the stimulus.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Reevaluate and Redirect the Stimulus Act of 2010”.

## § 2. OMB certification

Not later 15 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the Director of the Office of Management and Budget (referred to in this Act as the “Director”) shall provide to Congress—

- **(1)** a list of programs that have unobligated stimulus funds provided under the American Reinvestment and Recovery Act of 2009 and the amounts that are unobligated; and
- **(2)** a list of stimulus funds that remain unobligated that the Director recommends be redirected toward more effective programs to either assist displaced workers or spur job creation in 2010 with a breakdown of the amounts of unobligated funds that could be reprogrammed by program.
