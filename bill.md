# S. 2687

> To extend and modify the temporary suspension of duty on Aluminum tris (O-ethylphosphonate).

## § 1. Aluminum tris (O-ethylphosphonate)

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.01.73 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to Aluminum tris (O-ethylphosphonate)) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “Free” and inserting “0.4%”;
  - **(2)** by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2011”; and
  - **(3)** by striking the article description and inserting “Aluminum tris(O-ethylphosphonate) (CAS No. 39148–24–8) (provided for in subheadings 2920.90.50 and 3808.92.50) whether or not mixed with application adjuvants”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
