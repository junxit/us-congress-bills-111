# H.R. 5924

> To repeal section 929I of the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “SEC Freedom of Information Restoration Act”.

## § 2. Repeal

Section 929I of the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act (Public Law 111–203) is repealed, and the provisions of law amended or repealed by such Act are restored or revived as if such Act had not been enacted.
