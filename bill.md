# H.R. 2433

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the deduction for State and local sales taxes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “State Sales Equity Relief Act”.

## § 2. Extension of election to deduct state and local sales taxes

Subparagraph (I) of section 164(b)(5) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “January 1, 2010” and inserting “January 1, 2011”.
