# S. 3692

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to permanently extend the deductibility of mortgage insurance premiums.

## § 1. Permanent extension of deductibility of mortgage insurance premiums

- **(a)** *In general* Section 163(h)(3)(E) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking clause (iv).
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to amounts paid or accrued, or properly allocable to periods, after December 31, 2010.
