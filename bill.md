# H.R. 5311

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make permanent the treatment of municipal bonds guaranteed by Federal home loan banks as tax exempt bonds.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Community Development Financing Act of 2010”.

## § 2. Treatment of municipal bonds guaranteed by Federal home loan banks as tax exempt bonds made permanent

Clause (iv) of section 149(b)(3)(A) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “during the period beginning on the date of the enactment of this clause and ending on December 31, 2010” and inserting “on or after July 30, 2008”.
