# S. 2229

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on yarn of carded cashmere of 19.35 metric yarn count or higher.

## § 1. Yarn of carded cashmere of 19.35 metric yarn count or higher

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.03.02 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to yarn of carded cashmere of 19.35 metric yarn count or higher) is amended by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
