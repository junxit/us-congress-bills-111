# H.J. RES. 3

> Relating to the disapproval of obligations under the Emergency Economic Stabilization Act of 2008.

That Congress disapproves the obligation of any amount exceeding the amounts obligated as described in paragraphs (1) and (2) of section 115(a) of the Emergency Economic Stabilization Act of 2008.
