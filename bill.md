# H.R. 3251

> To repeal certain provisions of title 5, United States Code, relating to Federal employees’ official time and labor organization activities.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Federal Employee Accountability Act of 2009”.

## § 2. Repeal of certain provisions relating to official time

Subsections (a) and (c) of section 7131 of title 5, United States Code, are repealed.
