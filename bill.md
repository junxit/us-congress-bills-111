# S. 4017

> To amend the CDBG service cap.

## § 1. CDBG service cap adjustment

Section 105(a)(8) of the Housing and Community Development Act of 1974 (42 U.S.C. 5305(a)(8)) is amended by inserting before the semicolon the following: “, and except that in each of fiscal years 2011 and 2012, the limitation set forth in this paragraph shall be applied by substituting “25 percent” for “15 percent”;”.
