# H.R. 4406

> To render nationals of Israel eligible to enter the United States as nonimmigrant traders and investors.

## § 1. Nonimmigrant traders and investors from Israel

Israel shall be considered, for purposes of section 101(a)(15)(E) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1101(a)(15)(E)), to be a foreign state described in such section (other than clause (iii) of such section) if Israel extends reciprocal nonimmigrant treatment to nationals of the United States.
