# H.R. 1383

> To provide that, for purposes of certain Government facilities, the rate at which a Federal employee earns compensatory time for irregular or occasional overtime work shall be increased so as to permit greater parity with rates of overtime pay.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Air Force Depot Workers Fairness Act”.

## § 2. Increase in rate at which compensatory time accrues

- **(a)** *In general* In the case of Federal civilian employees described in subsection (b), section 5543 of title 5, United States Code, shall be applied as if such section had been amended—
  - **(1)** in subsections (a)(1) and (b), by striking “an equal amount of time” and inserting “one and one-half times the amount of time”; and
  - **(2)** in subsection (a)(2), by striking “equal to the amount of time” and inserting “equal to one and one-half times the amount of time”.
- **(b)** *Employees described* This Act applies in the case of Federal civilian employees serving at any of the following:
  - **(1)** The United States Air Force Depot at Tinker ALC (OK).
  - **(2)** The United States Air Force Depot at Ogden ALC (UT).
  - **(3)** The United States Air Force Depot at Warner Robbins ALC (GA).

## § 3. Effective date

This Act shall apply with respect to overtime work performed in any pay period beginning after the end of the 120-day period beginning on the date of the enactment of this Act.
