# S. 1229

> To reauthorize and improve the entrepreneurial development programs of the Small Business Administration, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Entrepreneurial Development Act of 2009”.

## § 3. Definitions

In this Act—

- **(1)** the terms Administration and Administrator mean the Small Business Administration and the Administrator thereof, respectively;
- **(2)** the term small business concern has the same meaning as in section 3 of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 632); and
- **(3)** the term small business development center means a small business development center described in section 21 of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 648).
