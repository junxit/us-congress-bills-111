# S. 3058

> To amend the Public Health Service Act to reauthorize the special diabetes programs for Type I diabetes and Indians under that Act.

## § 1. Reauthorization of special diabetes programs for Type I diabetes and Indians

- **(a)** *Special Diabetes Programs for Type I Diabetes* Section 330B(b)(2) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 254c–2(b)(2)) is amended—
  - **(1)** in subparagraph (B), by striking “and” at the end;
  - **(2)** in subparagraph (C), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
  - **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(D)** $200,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2012 through 2016.

- **(b)** *Special Diabetes Programs for Indians* Section 330C(c)(2) of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 254c–3(c)(2)) is amended—
  - **(1)** in subparagraph (B), by striking “and” at the end;
  - **(2)** in subparagraph (C), by striking the period at the end and inserting “; and”; and
  - **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(D)** $200,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2012 through 2016.
