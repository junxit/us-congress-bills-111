# H.R. 2557

> To name the Department of Veterans Affairs medical center in Portland, Oregon, as the “Barry L. Bell Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center”.

## § 1. Name of Department of Veterans Affairs medical center, Portland, Oregon

The Department of Veterans Affairs medical center located at 3710 SW U.S. Veterans Hospital Road, Portland, Oregon, shall after the date of the enactment of this Act be known and designated as the “Barry L. Bell Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center”. Any reference to such medical center in any law, regulation, map, document, record, or other paper of the United States shall be considered to be a reference to the Barry L. Bell Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center.
