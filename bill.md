# H.R. 1449

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to repeal the qualification standard for exterior windows, doors, and skylights.

## § 1. Repeal of qualification standard for exterior windows, doors, and skylights

- **(a)** *In general* Subsection (c) of section 25C of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended by the American Recovery and Reinvestment Tax Act of 2009, is amended by striking paragraph (4).
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to property placed in service after February 17, 2009.
