# S. 4037

> To impose a criminal penalty for unauthorized recording or distribution of images produced using advanced imaging technology during screenings of individuals at airports and upon entry to Federal buildings, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Security Screening Confidential Data Privacy Act”.
