# H.R. 3136

> To extend the program to provide grants for specified energy property in lieu of tax credits.

## § 1. Extension of program to provide grants for specified energy property in lieu of tax credits

- **(a)** *In general* Subsection (a) of section 1603 of the American Recovery and Reinvestment Tax Act of 2009 is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “2009 or 2010” both places it appears and by inserting “2009, 2010, 2011, or 2012”, and
  - **(2)** by striking “after 2010” in paragraph (2) and inserting “after 2012”.
- **(b)** *Deadline for submission of grant application* Subsection (j) of section 1603 of such Act is amended by striking “2011” and inserting “2013”.
