# H. RES. 995

> Of inquiry requesting the President to transmit to the House of Representatives all information in the possession of the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency relating to nutrient management of the Illinois River Watershed, Arkansas and Oklahoma.

That the House of Representatives requests the President to transmit to the House of Representatives, not later than 30 days after the date of adoption of this resolution, copies of all documents, including unredacted hard copies and electronic documents, in the possession of the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency relating to nutrient management of the Illinois River Watershed, Arkansas and Oklahoma.
