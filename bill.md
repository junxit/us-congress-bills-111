# S. 2669

> To extend temporarily the suspension of duty on electrically operated pencil sharpeners.

## § 1. Electrically operated pencil sharpeners

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.22.82 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to electrically operated pencil sharpeners) is amended by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
