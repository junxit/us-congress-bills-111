# H.R. 4944

> To repeal the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act and to replace such Act with incentives to encourage health insurance coverage, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title; table of contents

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Siding with America’s Patients Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of PPACA

Effective as of the enactment of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act, such Act is repealed, and the provisions of law amended or repealed by such Act are restored or revived as if such Act had not been enacted.
