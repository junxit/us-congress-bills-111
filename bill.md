# S. 527

> To amend the Clean Air Act to prohibit the issuance of permits under title V of that Act for certain emissions from agricultural production.

## § 1. Prohibition on permitting certain emissions from agricultural production

Section 502(f) of the Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. 7661a(f)) is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating paragraphs (1) through (3) as subparagraphs (A) through (C), respectively, and indenting the subparagraphs appropriately;
- **(2)** by striking the subsection designation and heading and all that follows through “No partial” and inserting the following:
  > - **(f)** *Prohibitions*
  >   - **(1)** *Partial permit programs* No partial

- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(2)** *Certain emissions from agricultural production* No permit shall be issued under a permit program under this title for any carbon dioxide, nitrogen oxide, water vapor, or methane emissions resulting from biological processes associated with livestock production.
