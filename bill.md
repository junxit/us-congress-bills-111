# H.R. 1767

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make the first-time homebuyer credit retroactive to the beginning of 2008 and to permanently extend the credit.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fair Housing Tax Credit Extension Act of 2009”.

## § 2. Application of first-time homebuyer credit

- **(a)** *In general* Subsection (h) of section 36 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended to read as follows:
  > - **(h)** *Application of section* This section shall only apply to a principal residence purchased by the taxpayer after December 31, 2007.

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to principal residences purchased after December 31, 2007.
