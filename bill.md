# S. 1533

> To provide an extension of public transportation programs authorized under the Safe, Accountable, Flexible, Efficient Transportation Equity Act: A Legacy for Users.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Public Transportation Extension Act of 2009”.

## § 2. Allocation of funds for planning programs
