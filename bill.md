# H.R. 5702

> To amend the District of Columbia Home Rule Act to reduce the waiting period for holding special elections to fill vacancies in the membership of the Council of the District of Columbia.

## § 1. Timing of Special Elections for Council of the District of Columbia

- **(a)** *Reduction of waiting period* Section 401(d)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act (sec. 1–204.01(d)(1), D.C. Official Code) is amended by striking “one hundred and fourteen days” and inserting “seventy days”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to vacancies occurring on or after the date of the enactment of this Act.
