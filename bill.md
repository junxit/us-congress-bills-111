# H.R. 1081

> To amend the Post-Katrina Emergency Management Reform Act of 2006 to extend the public assistance pilot program through December 31, 2009.

## § 1. Public assistance pilot program

- **(a)** *In general* Section 689j(d) of the Post-Katrina Emergency Management Reform Act of 2006 (6 U.S.C. 777(d)) is amended by striking “December 31, 2008” and inserting “December 31, 2009”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall take effect on January 1, 2009.
