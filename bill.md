# S. 3221

> To amend the Farm Security and Rural Investment Act of 2002 to extend the suspension of limitation on the period for which certain borrowers are eligible for guaranteed assistance.

## § 1. Suspension of limitation on period for which borrowers are eligible for guaranteed assistance

Section 5102 of the Farm Security and Rural Investment Act of 2002 (7 U.S.C. 1949 note; Public Law 107–171) is amended by striking “December 31, 2010” and inserting “December 31, 2012”.
