# S. 329

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the nonbusiness energy property credit for property placed in service during 2008.

## § 1. Extension of nonbusiness energy property credit for property placed in service during 2008

- **(a)** *In general* Subsection (g) of section 25C of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended to read as follows:
  > - **(g)** *Termination* This section shall not apply with respect to any property placed in service after December 31, 2009.

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to property placed in service after December 31, 2007.
