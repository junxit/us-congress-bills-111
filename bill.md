# H.R. 4005

> To place reasonable safeguards on the use of surveillance and other authorities under the USA PATRIOT Act, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Judicious Use of Surveillance Tools In Counterterrorism Efforts Act of 2009” or “JUSTICE Act” .
