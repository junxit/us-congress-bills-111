# H. CON. RES. 234

> Expressing the sense of Congress that there should be a freeze on new discretionary non-defense, non-homeland security, non-intelligence spending whenever there is a Federal budget deficit.

That it is the sense of Congress that whenever a Federal budget deficit exists for a fiscal year Congress should impose a freeze on new discretionary non-defense, non-homeland security, non-intelligence, non-veterans spending at then current levels until the Federal budget is in balance.
