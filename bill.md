# H.R. 3143

> To amend the Fort Peck Reservation Rural Water System Act of 2000, to extend the authorization of appropriations for that Act.

## § 1. Fort peck reservation rural water system, Montana

Section 9 of the Fort Peck Reservation Rural Water System Act of 2000 (Public Law 106–382; 114 Stat 1457), is amended by striking, “over a period of 10 fiscal years” each place it appears and inserting “through fiscal year 2015”.
