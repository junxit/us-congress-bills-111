# H.R. 4894

> To amend the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act to ensure appropriate treatment of Department of Veterans Affairs and Department of Defense health programs.

## § 1. Treatment of Department of Veterans Affairs and Department of Defense health programs

Subtitle G of title I of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act is amended by inserting the following new section:

> ## § 1564. Department of Veterans Affairs and Department of Defense health programs
>
> - **(a)** *Clarifications with respect to certain programs and authorities* Nothing in this Act or in the amendments made by this Act shall be construed as affecting any of the following:
>   - **(1)** Any authority under title 38, United States Code.
>   - **(2)** Any authority under chapter 55 of title 10, United States Code.
>   - **(3)** Any health care or health care benefit provided under the TRICARE program under chapter 55 of title 10, United States Code, or by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs under the laws administered by such Secretary.
> - **(b)** *Clarification with respect to minimum essential coverage* For purposes of this Act and the amendments made by this Act, the term minimum essential coverage includes the following:
>   - **(1)** Coverage provided under chapter 55 of title 10, United States Code.
>   - **(2)** Eligibility for health care provided by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs under title 38, United States Code.
