# H.R. 2224

> To amend section 7(a) of the Small Business Act to provide assistance to motor vehicle dealers, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Assistance to motor vehicle dealers

Section 7(a) of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 636(a)) is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating the second paragraph (32), as added by section 208 of the Military Reservist and Veteran Small Business Reauthorization and Opportunity Act of 2008 (Public Law 110–186; 122 Stat. 631), as paragraph (33); and
- **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(34)** *Motor vehicle dealers*
  >   - **(A)** *In general* The Administration may provide loans under this subsection to motor vehicle dealers for the purchase of motor vehicle inventory.
  >   - **(B)** *Amount* Notwithstanding any other limitation on the amount of a loan under this subsection, the maximum amount of a loan under this paragraph shall be $20,000,000 and the Administration may participate in a loan not exceeding such amount in the manner described in paragraph (2).
  >   - **(C)** *Motor vehicle* For purposes of this paragraph, the term motor vehicle includes passenger automobiles, tractor-trailers, motor homes, motorcycles, motorized heavy equipment, and motorized agricultural implements.
