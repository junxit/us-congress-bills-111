# H.R. 4917

> To amend part D of title XVIII of the Social Security Act to prohibit mid-year changes in the formularies of Medicare Part D plans.

## § 1. No mid-year formulary changes permitted

- **(a)** *In general* Section 1860D–4(b)(3)(E) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395w–104(b)(3)(E)) is amended by inserting “and only at the beginning of a plan year” before the period.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to plan years beginning on or after January 1, 2011.
