# H.R. 2469

> To amend the Controlled Substances Act to enhance criminal penalties for drug trafficking offenses relating to distribution of heroin, marihuana, and methamphetamine and distribution to and use of children, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Drug Trafficking Elimination Act of 2009”.
