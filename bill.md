# S. 3819

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to reduce the mileage threshold for the deduction for National Guard and Reservists overnight travel expenses.

## § 1. Reduction in mileage threshold for National Guard and Reservists overnight travel expenses deduction

- **(a)** *In general* Section 62(a)(2)(E) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “100 miles” and inserting “50 miles”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to amounts paid or incurred in taxable years beginning after December 31, 2010.
