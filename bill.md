# H.R. 4862

> To permit Members of Congress to administer the oath of allegiance to applicants for naturalization, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Congressional administration of the oath of allegiance

Section 337(a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1448(a)) is amended in the first sentence by inserting before “or a court with jurisdiction” the following: “, a Member of, or Delegate or Resident Commissioner to, the Congress,”.
