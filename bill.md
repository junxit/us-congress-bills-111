# S. 1889

> To extend the temporary duty suspension on glass bulbs, designed for sprinkler systems and other release devices.

## § 1. Certain glass thermo bulbs

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.24.26 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (releating to glass bulbs, designed for sprinkler systems and other release devices), is amended by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
