# H.R. 4577

> To direct the President, acting through the National Disaster Medical System, to reimburse States for expenses incurred in providing treatment for health conditions and illnesses resulting, directly or indirectly, from the earthquake in Haiti on January 12, 2010.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Emergency Health Services for Haitian Earthquake Victims Act of 2010”.

## § 2. Health services provided to Haitian earthquake victims

The President, acting through the National Disaster Medical System, shall make payments to reimburse States for expenses incurred in providing treatment for health conditions and illnesses resulting, directly or indirectly, from the earthquake in Haiti on January 12, 2010.
