# H.R. 3529

> To amend the Small Business Act to increase the maximum loan amount under the Express Loan Program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Increase of maximum loan amount under Express Loan Program

Section 7(a)(31)(D) of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 636(a)(31)(D)) is amended by striking “$350,000” and inserting “$2,000,000”.
