# H. RES. 205

> Providing for further consideration of the bill (H.R. 1106) to prevent mortgage foreclosures and enhance mortgage credit availability.

That during further consideration of the bill (H.R. 1106) to prevent mortgage foreclosures and enhance mortgage credit availability, pursuant to House Resolution 190, amendment number 1 printed in House Report 111–21 shall be considered as perfected by the modification printed in the report of the Committee on Rules accompanying this resolution.
