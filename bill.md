# S. 3836

> To make permanent the increase in the surety bond guarantee limits for the Small Business Administration.

## § 1. Surety bonds

Section 508(f) of division A of the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009 (15 U.S.C. 694a note) is repealed.
