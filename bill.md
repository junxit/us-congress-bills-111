# H.R. 4337

That the bill from the House of Representatives (H.R. 4337) entitled “An Act to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to modify certain rules applicable to regulated investment companies, and for other purposes.”, do pass with the following

*Strike all after the enacting clause and insert the following:*

## § 1. Short title, etc

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Regulated Investment Company Modernization Act of 2010”.
- **(b)** *Reference* Except as otherwise expressly provided, whenever in this Act an amendment or repeal is expressed in terms of an amendment to, or repeal of, a section or other provision, the reference shall be considered to be made to a section or other provision of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986.
