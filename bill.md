# S. 2717

> To extend the temporary reduction of duty on palm fatty acid distillate.

## § 1. Palm fatty acid distillate

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.11.32 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to palm fatty acid distillate) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “1%” and inserting “1.2%”; and
  - **(2)** in the effective period column, by striking the date contained therein and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) apply to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
