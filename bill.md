# S. RES. 370

> Relative to the death of Paula F. Hawkins, former United States Senator for the State of Florida.

## Preamble

- Whereas Paula F. Hawkins was a staunch consumer advocate and served the citizens of the State of Florida on its Public Service Commission for seven years, serving as its Chairman for three years;
- Whereas Paula F. Hawkins was instrumental in passing the Missing Children's Assistance Act of 1984 and worked to help establish the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children;
- Whereas Paula F. Hawkins served the people of Florida with distinction for 6 years in the United States Senate: Now, therefore, be it

That the Senate has heard with profound sorrow and deep regret the announcement of the death of the Honorable Paula F. Hawkins, former member of the United States Senate.
