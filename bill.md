# S. 302

> To authorize the International Boundary and Water Commission to reimburse State and local governments for expenses incurred by such governments in designing, constructing, and rehabilitating the Lower Rio Grande Valley Flood Control Project.

## § 1. Permitted reimbursements related to the Lower Rio Grande Valley Flood Control Project

The International Boundary and Water Commission is authorized to reimburse State and local governments for expenses incurred by such governments in designing, constructing, and rehabilitating the IBWC's Lower Rio Grande Valley Flood Control Project.
