# S. 3983

> To authorize the State of Ohio to reprogram grant funds received for intercity passenger rail service pursuant to title XII of the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009 for other transportation projects.

## § 1. Reprogramming of grants funds for transportation projects in Ohio

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the State of Ohio is authorized to use any grant funds received from the Federal Railroad Administration for capital projects described in subparagraphs (A) and (B) of section 24401(2) of title 49, United States Code, pursuant to appropriations under title XII of the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009 (Public Law 111–5), for any transportation project described in title 23 or 49, United States Code.
