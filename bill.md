# H.R. 3831

> To amend the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009 to eliminate the phase out of the Medicare hospice budget neutrality adjustment factor.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Hospice Support Act of 2009”.

## § 2. Elimination of phase out of Medicare hospice budget neutrality adjustment factor

Section 4301(a) of the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009 (Public Law 111–5) is amended—

- **(1)** by amending the heading to read as follows: “Elimination of phase out of Medicare hospice budget neutrality adjustment factor”; and
- **(2)** by striking “before October 1, 2009”.
