# H.R. 1164

> To amend title 5, United States Code, to exempt certain repayments under the Civil Service Retirement System from the requirement that they be made with interest.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “CSRS Retirement Repayment Technical Correction Act of 2009”.

## § 2. Exemption of certain repayments under the Civil Service Retirement System from the requirement that they be made with interest

- **(a)** *In general* Section 8334(d)(1) of title 5, United States Code, is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “(d)(1)” and inserting “(d)(1)(A)”; and
  - **(2)** by adding at the end the following:
    > - **(B)** No interest under subparagraph (A) shall be required in the case of any deposit to the extent that it represents the amount of any refund that was made to an employee or Member during the period beginning on October 1, 1990, and ending on February 28, 1991.

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) shall take effect as if included in the enactment of the Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1990 (Public Law 101–508).
