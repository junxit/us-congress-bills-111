# H.R. 4042

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the employer wage credit for employees who are active duty.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Small Business and Military Family Assistance Act of 2009”.

## § 2. Extension of employer wage credit for employees who are active duty members of the Uniformed Services

- **(a)** *In general* Subsection (f) of section 45P of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to employer wage credit for employees who are active duty members of the Uniformed Services) is amended by striking “December 31, 2009” and inserting “December 31, 2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to payments made after December 31, 2009.
