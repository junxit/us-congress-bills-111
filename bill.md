# H. RES. 965

> Repealing rule XXVIII of the Rules of the House of Representatives relating to the statutory limit on the public debt.

That rule XXVIII of the Rules of the House of Representatives (relating to the statutory limit on the public debt) is repealed and rule XXIX is redesignated as rule XXVIII.
