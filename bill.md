  - **(B)** in clause (ii)—

    - **(ii)** by striking “2016” and inserting “2010”; and
- **(2)** in section 105-—
  - **(A)** in paragraph (1)—
    - **(i)** in the paragraph heading, by striking “2016” and inserting “2010”; and
    - **(ii)** in subparagraph (A), by striking “2016” and inserting “2010”; and
  - **(B)** in paragraph (2)—
    - **(i)** in the paragraph heading, by striking “2017” and inserting “2011”; and
    - **(ii)** in subparagraph (A), by striking “2017” and inserting “2011”.
