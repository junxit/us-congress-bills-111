# S. 4

> To guarantee affordable, quality health coverage for all Americans, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Comprehensive Health Reform Act of 2009”.

## § 2. Sense of congress

It is the sense of Congress that Congress should enact, and the President should sign, legislation to guarantee health coverage, improve health care quality and disease prevention, and reduce health care costs for all Americans and the health care system.
