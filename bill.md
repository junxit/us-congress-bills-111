# H.R. 2004

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 4282 Beach Street in Akron, Michigan, as the “Akron Veterans Memorial Post Office”.

## § 1. Akron Veterans Memorial Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 4282 Beach Street in Akron, Michigan, shall be known and designated as the “Akron Veterans Memorial Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Akron Veterans Memorial Post Office”.
