# H.R. 2610

> To amend section 1886 of the Social Security Act to continue sole community hospital treatment for certain hospitals.

## § 1. Continuation of sole community hospital treatment for certain hospitals

- **(a)** *In general* Section 1886(d)(5)(B) of the Social Security Act (1395ww(d)(5)(B)) is amended by adding at the end the following new clause:
  > - **(vi)** If a hospital (in this clause referred to as the “new hospital”) begins operations on or after January 1, 2010, within 35 road miles of a hospital, that, as of the date of the new hospital beginning operations, is a sole community hospital (in this clause referred to as the “existing hospital”), the existing hospital shall continue to be treated as a sole community hospital if—
  >   - **(I)** the new hospital is privately owned or operated as a for-profit entity; and
  >   - **(II)** a portion of the new hospital’s ownership is attributable to physicians.

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to hospitals for cost reporting periods beginning on or after January 1, 2010.
