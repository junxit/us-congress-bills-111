# H.R. 4924

> To allow the Secretary of the Interior to clear a proposed project in the Lower St. Croix Wild and Scenic River, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Lower st. croix river

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, including the Wild and Scenic Rivers Act (16 U.S.C. 1271 et seq.), to the extent that funds are made available for this purpose by an Act other than this Act, the Secretary of the Interior shall find consistent with the Wild and Scenic Rivers Act the construction of a four-lane highway bridge over the Lower St. Croix River in accordance with the Section 7 Evaluation issued by the National Park Service in October 2005.
