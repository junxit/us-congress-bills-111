# H.R. 3392

> To prohibit any department or agency of the Federal Government from establishing a travel or conference policy that takes into account the perception of a location as a resort or vacation destination in determining the location for an event.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fair and Cost Effective Federal Travel Act of 2009”.

## § 2. Limitation on certain travel and conferences policies

No department or agency of the Federal Government may establish a travel or conference policy that takes into account the perception of a location as a resort or vacation destination in determining the location for an event.
