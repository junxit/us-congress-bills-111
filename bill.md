# H.R. 6395

> To amend the Alaska Natural Gas Pipeline Act with respect to certain requirements for construction job training program grants.

## § 1. Alaska Pipeline Construction Training Program

Section 113 of the Alaska Natural Gas Pipeline Act (15 U.S.C. 720k) is amended by striking subsection (b) and redesignating subsection (c) as subsection (b).
