# S. 2748

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend for one year the employer wage credit for employees who are active duty members of the uniformed services.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Small Business and Military Family Assistance Act of 2009”.

## § 2. Employer wage credit for employees who are active duty members of the uniformed services

- **(a)** *In general* Subsection (f) of section 45P of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “December 31, 2009” and inserting “December 31, 2010”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to payments made after December 31, 2009.
