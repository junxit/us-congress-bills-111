# H.R. 4863

> To increase the annual amount authorized for emergency assistance under the Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Act of 1981.

## § 1. Emergency assistance

Section 2602(e) of the Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Act of 1981 (42 U.S.C. 8621(e)) is amended by striking “$600,000,000” and inserting “$750,000,000”.
