# H.R. 3965

> To require full and complete public disclosure of the terms of home mortgages held by Members of Congress.

## § 1. Requiring Mortgage Disclosure

Section 102(a)(4)(A) of the Ethics in Government Act of 1978 (5 U.S.C. App) is amended by inserting after “spouse” the following: “, except that this exception shall not apply to a reporting individual described in section 101(f)(9)”.
