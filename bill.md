# H.R. 2436

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the charitable contributions deduction for food inventory, book inventory, and computer technology and equipment.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Charitable Goods Donation Act”.

## § 2. Extension of charitable contributions deduction for food inventory, book inventory, and computer technology and equipment

- **(a)** *Contributions of food inventory* Clause (iv) of section 170(e)(3)(C) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “December 31, 2009” and inserting “December 31, 2010”.
- **(b)** *Contributions of book inventory to public schools* Clause (iv) of section 170(e)(3)(D) of such Code is amended by striking “December 31, 2009” and inserting “December 31, 2010”.
- **(c)** *Contributions of computer technology and equipment for educational purposes* Subparagraph (G) of section 170(e)(6) of such Code is amended by striking “December 31, 2009” and inserting “December 31, 2010”.
