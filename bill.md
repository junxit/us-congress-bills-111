# H.R. 4246

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the alternative fuels credit for liquified petroleum gas through 2010.

## § 1. Alternative fuels credit for liquified petroleum gas extended through 2010

- **(a)** *Alternative fuel credit* Paragraph (5) of section 6426(d) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by inserting “December 31, 2010, in the case of any sale or use involving liquified petroleum gas and” before “September 30, 2014”.
- **(b)** *Alternative fuel mixture credit* Paragraph (3) of section 6426(e) of such Code is amended by inserting “December 31, 2010, in the case of any sale or use involving liquified petroleum gas and” before “September 30, 2014”.
