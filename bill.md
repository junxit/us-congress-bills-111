# S. 1882

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on certain ethylene-vinyl acetate copolymers.

## § 1. Certain ethylene-vinyl acetate copolymers

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.24.55 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to ethylene-vinyl acetate copolymers) is amended by striking “12/31/2009” and by inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
