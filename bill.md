# H.R. 3899

> To extend temporarily the duty on 1,3-bis(4-aminophenoxy)benzene (RODA).

## § 1. 1,3-bis(4-aminophenoxy)benzene (RODA)

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.05.15 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to 1,3-bis(4-aminophenoxy)benzene (RODA)) is amended by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2012”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section applies with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
