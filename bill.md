# H.R. 794

> To prohibit the use of funds to transfer enemy combatants detained by the United States at Naval Station, Guantanamo Bay, Cuba, to the Florence Federal Correctional Complex in Colorado, or to construct facilities for such enemy combatants at such location.

## § 1. Prohibition on use of funds to transfer individuals detained at Guantanamo Bay, Cuba, to the Florence Federal Correctional Complex in Colorado, or to construct facilities for such individuals at such location

None of the funds appropriated or otherwise made available to any Federal department or agency may be used—

- **(1)** to transfer any enemy combatant detained by the United States at Naval Station, Guantanamo Bay, Cuba, to the Florence Federal Correctional Complex, Colorado; or
- **(2)** to build, modify, or enhance any facility at the Florence Federal Correctional Complex, Colorado, for the purpose of housing any such enemy combatant.
