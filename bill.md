# S. RES. 704

> To authorize the printing of a revised edition of the Senate Election Law Guidebook.

That the Committee on Rules and Administration shall prepare a revised edition of the Senate Election Law Guidebook, Senate Document 109–10, and that such document shall be printed as a Senate document.

## § 2.

There shall be printed, beyond the usual number, 500 additional copies of the document specified in the first section for the use of the Committee on Rules and Administration.
