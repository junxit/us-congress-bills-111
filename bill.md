# H.J. RES. 66

> Disapproving a rule submitted by the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency relating to endangerment and cause or contribute findings for greenhouse gases under section 202(a) of the Clean Air Act.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency relating to endangerment and cause or contribute findings for greenhouse gases under section 202(a) of the Clean Air Act (74 Fed. Reg. 66496 (December 15, 2009)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
