# H.R. 3561

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to increase the amount of educational assistance provided to certain veterans for flight training.

## § 1. Assistance for flight training

Subsection (e)(1) of section 3032 of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “60 percent” and inserting “75 percent”.
