# H.R. 1541

> To provide for an additional temporary extension of programs under the Small Business Act and the Small Business Investment Act of 1958, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Extension

- **(a)** *In general* Section 1 of the Act entitled “An Act to extend temporarily certain authorities of the Small Business Administration”, approved October 10, 2006 (Public Law 109-316; 120 Stat. 1742), as most recently amended by section 1 of Public Law 110-235 (122 Stat. 1552), is amended by striking “March 20, 2009” in each place it appears and inserting “July 31, 2009”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall take effect on March 19, 2009.
