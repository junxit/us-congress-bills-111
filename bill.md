# H.R. 3722

> To amend the Small Business Investment Act of 1958 to improve the New Markets Venture Capital and Renewable Fuel Capital Investment Programs, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Enhanced New Markets and Expanded Investment in Renewable Energy for Small Manufacturers Act of 2009”.
