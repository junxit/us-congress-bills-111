# S. 201

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the additional standard deduction for real property taxes for nonitemizers for 2010.

## § 1. Extension of additional standard deduction for real property taxes for nonitemizers

- **(a)** *In general* Subparagraph (C) of section 63(c)(1) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “or 2009” and inserting “2009, or 2010”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after December 31, 2008.
