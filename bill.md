# H. RES. 1510

> Providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 4636) to prohibit United States assistance to foreign countries that oppose the position of the United States in the United Nations.

That upon the adoption of this resolution the House shall immediately proceed to consider in the House the bill (H.R. 4636) to prohibit United States assistance to foreign countries that oppose the position of the United States in the United Nations. All points of order against the bill and against its consideration are waived. The bill shall be considered as read. The previous question shall be considered as ordered on the bill to final passage without intervening motion except: (1) one hour of debate equally divided and controlled by Representative Gohmert of Texas or his designee and an opponent; and (2) one motion to recommit.
