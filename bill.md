# H. RES. 1579

> Establishing an earmark moratorium for fiscal year 2011.

That

- **(a)** it shall not be in order to consider—
  - **(1)** a bill or joint resolution reported by any committee, or any amendment thereto or conference report thereon, that includes a congressional earmark or limited tax benefit; or
  - **(2)** a bill or joint resolution not reported by any committee, or any amendment thereto or conference report thereon, that includes a congressional earmark or limited tax benefit.
- **(b)** For the purposes of this resolution, the terms “congressional earmark” and “limited tax benefit” have the meaning given those terms in clause 9 of rule XXI of the Rules of the House of Representatives.
- **(c)** The point of order under subsection (a) shall only apply to legislation providing or authorizing discretionary budget authority, credit authority, or other spending authority, or providing a Federal tax deduction, credit, or exclusion in fiscal year 2011.
- **(d)** This resolution shall not apply to any authorization of appropriations to a Federal entity if such authorization is not specifically targeted to a State, locality, or congressional district.

## § 2.

The Committee on Rules may not report a rule or order that would waive the point of order set forth in the first section of this resolution.
