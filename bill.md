# S. 1510

That the bill from the Senate (S. 1510) entitled “An Act to transfer statutory entitlements to pay and hours of work authorized by the District of Columbia Code for current members of the United States Secret Service Uniformed Division from the District of Columbia Code to the United States Code.”, do pass with the following

*Strike out all after the enacting clause and insert:*

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “United States Secret Service Uniformed Division Modernization Act of 2010”.
