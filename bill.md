# H.R. 2654

> To extend temporarily the suspension of duty on polyethylene HE1878.

## § 1. Polyethylene HE1878

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.10.34 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to polyethylene HE1878) is amended by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2012”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
