# H.R. 5375

> To amend the Tariff Act of 1930 relating to de minimis entries.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Customs De Minimis Adjustment Act of 2010”.

## § 2. De minimis entries

Section 321(a)(2)(C) of the Tariff Act of 1930 (19 U.S.C. 1321(a)(2)(C)) is amended by striking “$200” and inserting “$1,000”.
