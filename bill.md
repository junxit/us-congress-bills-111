# H.R. 1170

> To amend chapter 21 of title 38, United States Code, to establish a grant program to encourage the development of new assistive technologies for specially adapted housing.

## § 1. Specially Adapted Housing assistive technology grant program

- **(a)** *In general* Chapter 21 of title 38, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new section:
  > ## § 2108. Specially adapted housing assistive technology grant program
  >
  > - **(a)** *Establishment* The Secretary shall carry out a program to make grants to encourage the development of new assistive technologies for specially adapted housing.
  > - **(b)** *Application* A person seeking a grant under the program under this section shall submit to the Secretary an application for the grant in such form and manner as the Secretary shall specify for purposes of the program.
  > - **(c)** *Grant funds*
  >   - **(1)** *Amount* The amount of each grant awarded under the program under this section shall be an amount of not more than $200,000 per year.
  >   - **(2)** *Time of award* The Secretary shall award a grant under the program under this section not later than October 1 of each year in which the Secretary carries out the program.
  > - **(d)** *Use of funds*
  >   - **(1)** *In general* A person awarded a grant under the program under this section shall use amounts under the grant to develop assistive technologies for use in specially adapted housing.
  >   - **(2)** *Patents* If a person awarded a grant under the program under this section is awarded a patent related to assistive technology developed with amounts under the grant, the Secretary shall retain not less than a 30 percent interest in such patent.
  > - **(e)** *Report* Not later than March 1 of each year, the Secretary shall submit to Congress a report containing information related to each grant awarded under the program under this section in the preceding year, including—
  >   - **(1)** the name of the grant recipient;
  >   - **(2)** the amount of the grant; and
  >   - **(3)** the goal of the grant.
  > - **(f)** *Funding* From amounts appropriated to the Department for Medical Services for each fiscal year, $2,000,000 shall be available for each such fiscal year for the purposes of the program under this section.
