# S. 2001

> To extend temporarily the suspension of duty on certain high tenacity rayon filament yarn.

## § 1. Certain high tenacity rayon filament yarn

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.05.07 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to high tenacity single yarn of viscose rayon with a decitex equal to or greater than 1,000) is amended by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
