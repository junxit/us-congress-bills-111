# S. 3950

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to provide for the application of a consistent Medicare part B premium for all Medicare beneficiaries for 2011.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Medicare Premium Fairness Act”.

## § 2. Medicare part B premium for 2011

Section 1839 of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395r) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a), by adding at the end the following new paragraph:
  > - **(5)** The monthly premium under this subsection for 2011 shall be the monthly premium under this subsection for 2009.
