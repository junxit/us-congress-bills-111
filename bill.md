# H.R. 3414

> To name the Department of Veterans Affairs temporary lodging facility in Indianapolis, Indiana, as the “Otis Bowen Comfort Home”.

## § 1. Name of Department of Veterans Affairs temporary lodging facility, Indianapolis, Indiana

The Department of Veterans Affairs temporary lodging facility in Indianapolis, Indiana, shall after the date of the enactment of this Act be known and designated as the “Otis Bowen Comfort Home”. Any reference to such temporary lodging facility in any law, regulation, map, document, record, or other paper of the United States shall be considered to be a reference to the Otis Bowen Comfort Home.
