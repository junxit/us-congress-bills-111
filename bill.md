# S. 2354

> To extend temporarily the suspension of duty on RC Retarder 1092.

## § 1. RC Retarder 1092

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.12.78 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to RC Retarder 1092) is amended by striking the date in the effective column period and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
