# H.R. 3966

> To amend the American Reinvestment and Recovery Act of 2009 to extend for 6 months the period of eligibility for COBRA premium assistance under such Act.

## § 1. 6-month extension of eligibility period for COBRA premium assistance under ARRA

Section 3001(a)(3)(A) of division B of the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009 (Public Law 111–5) is amended by striking “December 31, 2009” and inserting “June 30, 2010”.
