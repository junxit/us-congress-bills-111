# H.R. 435

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide a 5-year extension of the credit for electricity produced from certain renewable resources.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Renewable Energy Production Tax Credit Extension Act of 2009”.

## § 2. 5-year extension of credit for electricity produced from certain renewable resources

Section 45(d) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to qualified facilities) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “2010” each place it appears in paragraphs (1) and (8) and inserting “2015”,
- **(2)** by striking “2011” each place it appears in paragraphs (2), (3), (4), (5), (6), (7) and (9) and inserting “2016”, and
- **(3)** by striking “2012” in paragraph (11)(B) and inserting “2017”.
