# S. 3183

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the nonbusiness energy property credit to roofs with pigmented coatings which meet Energy Star program requirements.

## § 1. Extension of nonbusiness energy property credit to roofs with pigmented coatings which meet Energy Star program requirements

- **(a)** *In general* Section 25C(c)(1) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by inserting “or pigmented coatings” after “appropriate cooling granules”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to property placed in service after the date of the enactment of this Act.
