# H.R. 4074

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to eliminate the temporary increase in unemployment tax.

## § 1. Repeal of temporary increase in unemployment tax

- **(a)** *In general* Section 3301 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “equal to—” and all that follows through “of the total wages” and inserting the following: “equal to 6.0 percent of the total wages”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to wages paid during months beginning after the date of the enactment of this Act.
