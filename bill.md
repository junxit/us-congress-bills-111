# H.R. 2063

> To amend the Emergency Economic Stabilization Act of 2008 to use repaid Troubled Asset Relief Program funds to pay down the public debt, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Public debt reduction

- **(a)** *In general* Title I of the Emergency Economic Stabilization Act of 2008 (12 U.S.C. 5201 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following new section:
  > ## § 137. Public debt reduction
  >
  > - **(a)** *In general* Each time a financial institution makes a repayment of assistance provided under this title, the Secretary shall transfer such repayment to the special account established by section 3113(d) of title 31, United States Code.
  > - **(b)** *TARP authorization reduction* Each time a financial institution makes a repayment of assistance provided under this title, the authorization to purchase authority under section 115 shall be reduced by a corresponding dollar amount.
