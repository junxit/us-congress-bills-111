# S. 3190

> To reaffirm that the Small Business Reauthorization Act of 1997 does not limit a contracting officer's discretion regarding whether to make a contract available for award pursuant to any of the restricted competition programs authorized by the Small Business Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Small Business Programs Parity Act of 2010”.

## § 2. Small business contracting programs parity

Section 31(b)(2)(B) of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 657a(b)(2)(B)) is amended by striking “shall” and inserting “may”.
