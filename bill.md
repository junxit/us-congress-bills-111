# H.R. 6340

> To amend the Supplemental Appropriations Act, 2008 to modify the threshold rates of unemployment that must be met by a State in order to establish eligibility for fourth-tier emergency unemployment compensation with respect to such State.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fairness in Unemployment Act”.

## § 2. Amendments

- **(a)** *In general* Section 4002(e)(2) of the Supplemental Appropriations Act, 2008 (26 U.S.C. 3304 note) is amended—
  - **(1)** in subparagraph (A), by striking “6” and inserting “4”; and
  - **(2)** in subparagraph (B), by striking “8.5” and inserting “7.0”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) shall apply to individuals exhausting third-tier emergency unemployment compensation on or after the date of the enactment of this Act.
