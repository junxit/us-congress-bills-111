# H.R. 2604

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make permanent the additional standard deduction for real property taxes for nonitemizers.

## § 1. Additional standard deduction for real property taxes for nonitemizers made permanent

- **(a)** *In general* Subparagraph (C) of section 63(c)(1) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “in the case of any taxable year beginning in 2008 or 2009,”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after December 31, 2008.
