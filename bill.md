# S. RES. 567

> To elect Daniel K. Inouye, a Senator from the State of Hawaii, to be President pro tempore of the Senate of the United States.

That Daniel K. Inouye, a Senator from the State of Hawaii, be, and he is hereby, elected President of the Senate pro tempore.
