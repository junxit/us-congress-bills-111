# H.R. 2508

> To extend patent numbered 5,180,715 for a period of 2 years.

## § 1. Extension of patent term

The Under Secretary of Commerce for Intellectual Property and Director of the United States Patent and Trademark Office shall, when patent numbered 5,180,715 (relating to irrigation of internal bladder surfaces in mammals with sodium pentosanpolysulfate) expires, extend such patent for a period of 2 years, with all the rights pertaining to such patent.
