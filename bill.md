# S. 2454

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on mixtures or coprecipitates of yttrium oxide and europium oxide.

## § 1. Mixtures or coprecipitates of yttrium oxide and europium oxide

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.23.04 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to mixtures or coprecipitates of yttrium oxide and europium oxide) is amended by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
