# S. 2147

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on certain AC electric motors of an output exceeding 37.5 W but not exceeding 72 W.

## § 1. Certain AC electric motors of an output exceeding 37.5 W but not exceeding 72 W

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.85.09 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to certain AC electric motors of an output exceeding 37.5 W but not exceeding 72 W) is amended by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
