# H.R. 4766

> To permanently extend the Protecting Tenants at Foreclosure Act of 2009.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Permanently Protecting Tenants at Foreclosure Act of 2010”.

## § 2. Repeal of sunset provision

Section 704 of the Protecting Tenants at Foreclosure Act of 2009 (12 U.S.C. 5201 note; 12 U.S.C. 5220 note; 42 U.S.C. 1437f note) is hereby repealed.
