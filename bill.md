# S. 3762

> To reinstate funds to the Federal Land Disposal Account.

## § 1. Federal Land Disposal Account

Notwithstanding section 206(f) of the Federal Land Transaction Facilitation Act (43 U.S.C. 2305(f)), any balance remaining in the Federal Land Disposal Account on July 24, 2010, shall be reinstated and available for expenditure in accordance with section 206(b) of that Act (43 U.S.C. 2305(b)), to remain available until expended.
