# H. RES. 48

> Amending the Rules of the House of Representatives to establish the Committee on Indian Affairs.

## § 1. Committee on Indian Affairs

- **(a)** *Establishment* Clause 1 of rule X of the Rules of the House of Representatives is amended—
  - **(1)** by redesignating paragraphs (k) through (t) as paragraphs (l) through (u); and
  - **(2)** by inserting after paragraph (j) the following new paragraph:
    > - **(k)** Committee on Indian Affairs.
    >   - **(1)** Native Americans generally, including the care and allotment of Native American lands and general and special measures relating to claims that are paid out of Native American funds.
    >   - **(2)** Relations of the United States with Native Americans and Native American tribes.

- **(b)** *Conforming Amendment* Clause 1(l) of rule X of the Rules of the House of Representatives is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking subparagraph (8) and redesignating subparagraphs (9) through (19) as subparagraphs (8) through (18); and
  - **(2)** by striking subparagraph (20) and redesignating subparagraph (21) as subparagraph (19).
