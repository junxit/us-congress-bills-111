# H.R. 1892

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 102 North Main Street in Cedarville, Ohio, as the “William “Brent” Turner Post Office”.

## § 1. William “Brent” Turner Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 102 North Main Street in Cedarville, Ohio, shall be known and designated as the “William “Brent” Turner Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “William “Brent” Turner Post Office”.
