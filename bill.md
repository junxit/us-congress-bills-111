# H.R. 6517

> To extend trade adjustment assistance and certain trade preference programs, to amend the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States to modify temporarily certain rates of duty, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Omnibus Trade Act of 2010”.

## § 2. Organization of Act into divisions; table of contents

- **(a)** *Divisions* This Act is organized into three divisions as follows:
  - **(1)** Division A—Extension of Trade Adjustment Assistance and certain trade preference programs.
  - **(2)** Division B—Tariff and related provisions.
