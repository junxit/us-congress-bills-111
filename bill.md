# H.R. 2048

> To authorize appropriations for the Office of Detention and Removal of United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement.

## § 1. Authorization of appropriations for Office of Detention and Removal

There is authorized to be appropriated $5,000,000,000 to the Office of Detention and Removal of United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement.
