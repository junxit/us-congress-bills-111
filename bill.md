# H.R. 5360

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to modify the standard of visual acuity required for eligibility for specially adapted housing assistance provided by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Blinded Veterans Adaptive Housing Improvement Act of 2010”.

## § 2. Modification of standard of visual acuity required for eligibility for specially adapted housing assistance provided by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs

- **(a)** *In general* Section 2101(b)(2)(A) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “5/200 visual acuity or less” and inserting “20/200 best corrected visual acuity or less or a peripheral field of 20 degrees or less”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to specially adapted housing assistance provided on or after the date of the enactment of this Act.
