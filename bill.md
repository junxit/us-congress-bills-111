# S. 2753

> To extend temporarily the suspension of duty on RSD 1235.

## § 1. RSD 1235

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.22.93 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to RSD 1235) is amended by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
