## § 2. Definitions

As used in this Act, the following definitions apply:

- **(1)** The term passenger motor vehicle means a motor vehicle (as defined in section 30102(a)(6) of title 49, United States Code) that is rated at less than 10,000 pounds gross vehicular weight. Such term does not include—
  - **(A)** a motorcycle;
  - **(B)** a trailer; or
  - **(C)** a low speed vehicle (as defined in section 571.3 in title 49, Code of Federal Regulations).
- **(2)** The term Secretary means the Secretary of Transportation, acting through the Administrator of the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration.
