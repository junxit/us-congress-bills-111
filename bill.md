# H.R. 1171

> To amend title 38, United States Code, to reauthorize the Homeless Veterans Reintegration Program for fiscal years 2010 through 2014.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Homeless Veterans Reintegration Program Reauthorization Act of 2009”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of appropriations for Homeless Veterans Reintegration Program

Section 2021(e)(1)(F) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking “2009” and inserting “2014”.
