# H.R. 1871

> To designate certain counties in the State of Arizona as high-intensity drug trafficking areas.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Southwest Border Narcotics Reduction Act of 2009”.

## § 2. Designation of high-intensity drug trafficking areas in Arizona

The counties of Apache, Gila, Coconino, Yavapai, Navajo, Graham, and Greenlee in the State of Arizona are designated as high-intensity drug trafficking areas and shall be part of the Southwest Border high-intensity drug trafficking area under the program administered by the Office of National Drug Control Policy.
