# H.R. 4111

> To prohibit the prosecution of unprivileged enemy combatants by the Department of Justice.

## § 1. Prohibition on prosecution of unprivileged enemy combatants by Department of Justice

- **(a)** *In general* The Department of Justice shall not prosecute any unprivileged enemy combatant for any offense.
- **(b)** *Unprivileged enemy combatant defined* The term “unprivileged enemy combatant” has the meaning given that term in section 948a(7) of title 10, United States Code.
