# H. RES. 450

> Waiving a requirement of clause 6(a) of rule XIII with respect to consideration of certain resolutions reported from the Committee on Rules.

That the requirement of clause 6(a) of rule XIII for a two-thirds vote to consider a report from the Committee on Rules on the same day it is presented to the House is waived with respect to any resolution reported on the legislative day of May 19, 2009, providing for consideration or disposition of the bill (S. 896) to prevent mortgage foreclosures and enhance mortgage credit availability.
