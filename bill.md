# H.R. 5316

> To direct the Secretary of Labor to establish an office in Anchorage, Alaska, under the Office of Workers’ Compensation Programs.

## § 1. Local Office of Workers’ Compensation Programs in Anchorage, Alaska

Not later than 180 days after the date of enactment of this Act, the Secretary of Labor shall establish an office in Anchorage, Alaska, to operate under the Department of Labor’s Office of Workers’ Compensation Programs.
