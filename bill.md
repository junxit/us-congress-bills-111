# H.R. 5865

> To amend title 44, United States Code, to prohibit the Archivist of the United States from making grants to preserve or publish non-Federal records.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Stop Wasting Archive Grants Act of 2010”.

## § 2. Prohibiting the Archivist of the United States from making grants to preserve or publish non-Federal records

Section 2504(e) of title 44, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end the following new sentence: “The Archivist may not make grants to preserve or publish non-Federal records.”
