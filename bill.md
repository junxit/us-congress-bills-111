# S. 2808

> To improve the Express Loan Program of the Small Business Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Express Loan Improvements Act of 2009”.

## § 2. Express Loan Program

- **(a)** *In general* Section 7(a)(31) of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 636(a)(31)) is amended—
  - **(1)** in subparagraph (A)(iii), by striking “50 percent” and inserting “75 percent”; and
  - **(2)** in subparagraph (D), by striking “$350,000” and inserting “$1,000,000”.
- **(b)** *Prospective repeal* Effective 2 years after the date of enactment of this Act, section 7(a)(31)(A)(iii) of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 636(a)(31)(A)(iii)) is amended by striking “75 percent” and inserting “50 percent”.
