# H. RES. 431

> Impeaching Samuel B. Kent, judge of the United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, for high crimes and misdemeanors.

That Samuel B. Kent, judge of the United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, is impeached for high crimes and misdemeanors.
