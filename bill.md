# S. 2314

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on 4,4′-Oxydiphthalic anhydride.

## § 1. 4,4′-Oxydiphthalic anhydride

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.05.13 of subchapter II of chapter 99 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to 4,4′-Oxydiphthalic anhydride) is amended by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section applies with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
