# H.R. 1854

> To amend the Water Resources Development Act of 1992 to modify an environmental infrastructure project for Big Bear Lake, California.

## § 1. Big Bear Lake, California

Section 219(f)(84) of the Water Resources Development Act of 1992 (121 Stat. 1259) is amended to read as follows:

> - **(84)** *Big Bear Lake, California* $9,000,000 for water supply infrastructure improvements for Big Bear Lake, California.
