# H.R. 1538

> To repeal the small business competitiveness demonstration program.

## § 1. Repeal of small business competitiveness demonstration program

Sections 701 through 722 of the Business Opportunity Development Reform Act of 1988 (Public Law 100–656; 15 U.S.C. 644 note) are repealed.
