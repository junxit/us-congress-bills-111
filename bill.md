# H.R. 6325

> To amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act and the Federal Meat Inspection Act to require that food that contains bioengineered products be labeled accordingly, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Consumer Right To Know Food Labeling Act of 2010”.
