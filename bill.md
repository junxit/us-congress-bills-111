# H.R. 1213

> To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to provide that the Medicare initial preventive physical examination not be required for a referral with respect to ultrasound screening for abdominal aortic aneurysms and to provide for such screening with respect to at-risk Medicare beneficiaries between the ages of 65 and 75.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Screening Abdominal Aortic Aneurysms Very Efficiently Act of 2009”.

## § 2. Medicare coverage of ultrasound screening for abdominal aortic aneurysms

- **(a)** *In general* Section 1861 of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1395x) is amended—
  - **(1)** in subsection (s)(2)(AA), by amending clause (i) to read as follows:
    > - **(i)** who is at least 65 years of age and not greater than 75 years of age;

  - **(2)** in subsection (ww)(2), by striking subparagraph (L) and redesignating subparagraphs (M) and (N) as subparagraphs (L) and (M), respectively.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to services furnished on or after January 1, 2010.
