# S. 3609

> To extend the temporary authority for the performance of medical disability examinations by contract physicians for the Department of Veterans Affairs.

## § 1. Extension of authority for performance of medical disability examinations by contract physicians

Section 704(c) of the Veterans Benefits Act of 2003 (Public Law 108–183; 38 U.S.C. 5101 note) is amended by striking “December 31, 2010” and inserting “December 31, 2012”.
