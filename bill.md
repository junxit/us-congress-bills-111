# H.R. 2632

> To amend title 4, United States Code, to encourage the display of the flag of the United States on National Korean War Veterans Armistice Day.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Korean War Veterans Recognition Act”.

## § 2. Display of flag on National Korean War Veterans Armistice Day

Section 6(d) of title 4, United States Code, is amended by inserting “National Korean War Veterans Armistice Day, July 27;” after “July 4;”.
