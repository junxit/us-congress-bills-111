# H.R. 4281

> To amend the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 and title 11, United States Code, to provide necessary reforms for employee pension benefit plans.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Employees’ Pension Security Act of 2009”.
