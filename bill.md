# H.R. 5459

> To increase the limits on liability under the Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act Amendments Act of 2010”.

## § 2. Remedies and penalties under the Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act

- **(a)** *Civil penalties* Section 24(b) of the Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act (43 U.S.C. 1350(b)) is amended—
  - **(1)** in the first sentence of paragraph (1), by striking “$20,000” and inserting “$75,000”; and
  - **(2)** in paragraph (2), by striking “may be assessed” and inserting “of not more than $150,000 for each day of the continuance of the failure shall be assessed”.
- **(b)** *Criminal penalties* The first sentence of section 24(c) of the Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act (43 U.S.C. 1350(c)) is amended by striking “$100,000” and inserting “$10,000,000”.
- **(c)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) take effect on April 15, 2010.
