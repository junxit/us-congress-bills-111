# S. 1090

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide tax credit parity for electricity produced from renewable resources.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Renewable Energy Parity and Investment Remedy Act” or “REPAIR Act”.

## § 2. Tax credit parity for electricity produced from renewable resources

Subparagraph (A) of section 45(b)(4) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by inserting “and before 2010” after “any calendar year after 2003”.
