# H.R. 4486

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to treat distributions of debt securities in a tax free spin-off transaction in the same manner as distributions of cash or other property.

## § 1. Treatment of securities of a controlled corporation exchanged for assets in certain reorganizations

- **(a)** *In general* Section 361 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (relating to nonrecognition of gain or loss to corporations; treatment of distributions) is amended by adding at the end the following new subsection:
  > - **(d)** *Receipt of securities, etc., in exchange for assets in certain reorganizations* If—
  >   - **(1)** property is transferred to a corporation (hereinafter in this subsection referred to as the “controlled corporation”) pursuant to a plan of reorganization described in section 368(a)(1)(D), and
  >   - **(2)** pursuant to such plan of reorganization, stock or securities in the controlled corporation are distributed in a transaction which qualifies under section 355,

- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to distributions after December 31, 2009.
