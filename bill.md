# H.R. 5959

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to repeal the withholding of income taxes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Tax Education & Accountability Act” or the “TEA Act”.

## § 2. Termination of income tax withholding

- **(a)** *In general* Subchapter A of chapter 24 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by adding at the end the following new section:
  > ## § 3407. Termination of income tax withholding
  >
  > No amount shall be deducted and withheld as tax under this chapter with respect to any amounts paid on or after the first January 1 occurring after 1 year after the date of the enactment of this Act.
