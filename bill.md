# S. 2404

> To extend the temporary reduction of duty on acrylic or modacrylic filament tow.

## § 1. Acrylic or modacrylic filament tow

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.10.21 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to acrylic or modacrylic filament tow) is amended by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
