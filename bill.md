# H.R. 910

> For the relief of Judge John S. Unpingco.

## § 1. Satisfaction of age and service requirements

Judge John S. Unpingco of Piti, Guam, shall be deemed to have met the age requirement set forth in section 373(e) of title 28, United States Code, and the service requirement set forth in section 373(e)(1) of title 28 of such Code.
