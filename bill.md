# H.R. 3112

> To authorize the Secretary of the department in which the Coast Guard is operating to issue a certificate of documentation for operation in the coastwise trade for the vessel EQUULEUS.

## § 1. VESSEL EQUULEUS

Notwithstanding sections 55101, 55102, and 12112 of title 46, United States Code, the Secretary of the department in which the Coast Guard is operating may issue a certificate of documentation with a coastwise endorsement for the motor vessel EQUULEUS (IMO 5146914).
