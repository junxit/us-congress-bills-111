# H.R. 1394

> To amend the National and Community Service Act of 1990 to establish the Clean Energy Corps to mobilize young people to promote energy conservation and mitigate threats to the environment.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Clean Energy Corps Act”.
