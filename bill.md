# H.R. 3704

> To authorize a Department of Veterans Affairs major medical facility lease in Atlanta, Georgia.

## § 1. Authorization of major medical facility lease, Atlanta, Georgia

The Secretary of Veterans Affairs may carry out a major medical facility lease for a Specialty Care Clinic in Atlanta, Georgia, in an amount not to exceed $5,172,000.
