# H.R. 2396

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide for an extension of the employer wage credit for employees who are active duty members of the Uniformed Services.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Small Business Supporting our Troops Act of 2009”.

## § 2. Extension of employer wage credit for employees who are active duty members of the Uniformed Services

- **(a)** *In general* Subsection (f) of section 45P of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “December 31, 2009” and inserting “December 31, 2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply to amounts paid after December 31, 2009.
