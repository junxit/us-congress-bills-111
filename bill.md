# S. 2761

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the bonus depreciation deduction applicable to the Gulf Opportunity Zone for 2 additional years.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “GO Zone Bonus Depreciation Extension Act”.

## § 2. Extension of bonus depreciation deduction applicable to the Gulf Opportunity Zone for 2 additional years

- **(a)** *In general* Paragraph (6) of section 1400N(d) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “2010” each place it appears and inserting “2012”.
