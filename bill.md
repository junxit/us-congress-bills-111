# S. 1182

> To amend the Chinese Student Protection Act of 1992 to eliminate the offset in per country numerical level required under that Act.

## § 1. Elimination of offset in per country numerical level

Section 2 of the Chinese Student Protection Act of 1992 (8 U.S.C. 1255 note) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a), by striking “subsection (e)” and inserting “subsection (d)”;
- **(2)** by striking subsection (d); and
- **(3)** by redesignating subsection (e) as subsection (d).
