# S. 681

> To provide for special rules relating to assistance concerning the Greensburg, Kansas tornado.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Greensburg, Kansas Recovery Extension Act”.

## § 2. Availability of funds to address Greensburg, Kansas tornado

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, in the case of any national emergency grant that was made under section 173 of the Workforce Investment Act of 1998 (29 U.S.C. 2918) to address the effects of the May 4, 2007, Greensburg, Kansas tornado, funds made available for such grant shall remain available for expenditure through June 30, 2010.
