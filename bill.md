# H.R. 6041

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude income attributable to certain empowerment zone real property from gross income.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Bringing Business Back Act of 2010”.

## § 2. Incentives for certain empowerment zone real property

- **(c)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after the date of the enactment of this Act.
