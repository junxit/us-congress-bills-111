# S. 3443

> To amend the Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act to eliminate the 30-day time limit for approving exploration plans.

## § 1. Exploration plans for offshore drilling

Section 11(c)(1) of the Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act (43 U.S.C. 1340(c)(1)) is amended in the fourth sentence by striking “within thirty days of its submission,”.
