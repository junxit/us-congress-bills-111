# S. 1909

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on strontium magnesium phosphate-tin doped inorganic products.

## § 1. Strontium magnesium phosphate-tin doped inorganic products

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.22.68 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to strontium magnesium phosphate-tin doped inorganic products) is amended by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
