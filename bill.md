# H.R. 2930

> To enhance the primary care workforce through modifications to the medical residency training programs and use of qualified teaching health centers and through State primary care scholarship and loan repayment programs.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Primary Care Training Enhancement Act”.
