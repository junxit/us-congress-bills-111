# H.J. RES. 72

> Disapproving the action of the District of Columbia Council in approving the Religious Freedom and Civil Marriage Equality Amendment Act of 2009.

That the Congress disapproves of the action of the District of Columbia Council described as follows: The Religious Freedom and Civil Marriage Equality Amendment Act of 2009 (D.C. Act 18–248), approved by the District of Columbia Council on December 15, 2009, and transmitted to Congress pursuant to section 602(c) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act on January 5, 2010.
