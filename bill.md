# S. 1715

> To amend the United States International Broadcasting Act of 1994 to extend the authority of the Broadcasting Board of Governors to make grants for the purpose of operating Radio Free Asia.

## § 1. Extension of grant authority for Radio Free Asia

Section 309(f) of the United States International Broadcasting Act of 1994 (22 U.S.C. 6208(f)) is amended by striking “September 30, 2009” and inserting “September 30, 2010”.
