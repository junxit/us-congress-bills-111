# S. 2241

> To extend and modify the temporary reduction of duty on artichokes, prepared or preserved by vinegar or acetic acid.

## § 1. Artichokes, prepared or preserved by vinegar or acetic acid

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.03.90 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to artichokes prepared or preserved by vinegar) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “7.9%” and inserting “6.64%”; and
  - **(2)** by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
