# S. 2353

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on Rhenogran CLD–80.

## § 1. Rhenogran CLD–80

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.12.74 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to Rhenogran CLD–80) is amended by striking the date in the effective column period and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
