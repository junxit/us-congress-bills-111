# H.R. 1332

> To amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act with respect to the safety of the food supply.

## § 1. Short title; references; table of contents

- **(a)** *Short title* This Act may be cited as the “Safe Food Enforcement, Assessment, Standards, and Targeting Act of 2009” or as the “Safe FEAST Act of 2009” .
- **(b)** *References* Except as otherwise specified, whenever in this Act an amendment is expressed in terms of an amendment to a section or other provision, the reference shall be construed to be made to a section or other provision of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 301 et seq.).
