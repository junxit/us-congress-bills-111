# H.R. 4275

> To designate the annex building under construction for the Elbert P. Tuttle United States Court of Appeals Building in Atlanta, Georgia, as the “John C. Godbold United States Judicial Administration Building”.

## § 1. Designation

The annex building under construction for the United States courthouse located 56 Forsyth Street in Atlanta, Georgia, know as the Elbert P. Tuttle United States Court of Appeals Building, shall be known and designated as the “John C. Godbold United States Judicial Administration Building”.

## § 2. References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the annex building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “John C. Godbold United States Judicial Administration Building”.
