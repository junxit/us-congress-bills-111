# H. RES. 1406

> Directing the Secretary of the Interior to transmit to the House of Representatives certain information relating to the potential designation of National Monuments.

That the Secretary of the Interior is directed to transmit to the House of Representatives, not later than 14 days after the date of the adoption of this resolution, copies of all Department of the Interior documents, maps, records (including electronic records), communications and other information dating from July 1, 2009, and later referring to or relating to—

- **(1)** the document containing “Attachment 4 Prospective Conservation Designation: National Monument Designations under the Antiquities Act” and marked “Internal Draft—NOT FOR RELEASE”, including that document in full, all attachments in full, and all iterations of that document, and related similar documents.
