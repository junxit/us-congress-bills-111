# H.R. 4293

> To amend the Public Works and Economic Development Act of 1965 to maximize the efficiency in administering governmental functions.

## § 1. Efficiency in government

Section 207 of the Public Works and Economic Development Act of 1965(a) (42 U.S.C. 3147(a)(1)) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (1) by inserting “maximizing the efficiency in administering governmental functions and” after “grants for”; and
- **(2)** in paragraph (2)—
  - **(A)** by redesignating subparagraphs (H) and (I) as subparagraphs (I) and (J), respectively; and
  - **(B)** by inserting after subparagraph (G) the following:
    > - **(H) developing and conducting or implementing county or municipal government efficiency assessments;)**
