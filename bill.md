# H.R. 950

> To amend chapter 33 of title 38, United States Code, to increase educational assistance for certain veterans pursuing a program of education offered through distance learning.

## § 1. Increase of educational assistance for certain veterans pursuing a program of education offered through distance learning

Section 3313(c)(1)(B)(i) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking the following: “(other than, in the case of assistance under this section only, a program of education offered through distance learning)”.
