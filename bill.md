# S. 3282

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the work opportunity tax credit for employers of certain veterans.

## § 1. Extension of work opportunity credit for unemployed veterans

- **(a)** *In general* Paragraph (14) of section 51(d) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “hired in 2009 or 2010” in the heading,
  - **(2)** by striking “who begins work for the employer during 2009 or 2010” in subparagraph (A),
  - **(3)** by inserting “who begins work for the employer during 2009, 2010, 2011, or 2012, and” after “without regard to clause (ii) thereof)” in subparagraph (B)(i), and
  - **(4)** by inserting “who begins work for the employer during 2009 or 2010 and” after “any individual” in subparagraph (B)(ii).
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to individuals whose hiring date (as defined in section 51(d)(11) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986) is after December 31, 2010.
