# S. 39

> To repeal section 10(f) of Public Law 93–531, commonly known as the “Bennett Freeze”.

## § 1. Repeal of the Bennett Freeze

Section 10(f) of Public Law 93–531 (25 U.S.C. 640d–9(f)) is repealed.
