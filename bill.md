# H.R. 5489

> To amend section 14102(a)(1)(A) of title 40, United States Code, to provide that Bullock County, Alabama, is included in the definition of the Appalachian region for purposes of Appalachian regional development.

## § 1. Amendment to include Bullock County, Alabama

Section 14102(a)(1)(A) of title 40, United States Code, is amended by inserting “Bullock,” after “Blount,”.
