# S. 2624

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on magnesium zinc aluminum hydroxide carbonate coated with stearic acid.

## § 1. Magnesium zinc aluminum hydroxide carbonate coated with stearic acid

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.24.13 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to magnesium zinc aluminum hydroxide carbonate coated with stearic acid) is amended—
  - **(1)** by amending the article description to read as follows: “Magnesium zinc aluminum hydroxide carbonate (CAS No. 169314–88–9) coated with stearic acid (CAS No. 57–11–4) (provided for in subheading 3812.30.90)”; and
  - **(2)** in the effective period column, by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
