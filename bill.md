# H.R. 6350

> To amend title XIX of the Public Health Service Act to reauthorize the program under such title relating to lifespan respite care.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Lifespan Respite Care Reauthorization Act of 2010”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of lifespan respite care program

Section 2905 of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 300ii–4) is amended—

- **(1)** in paragraph (4), by striking “and” at the end;
- **(2)** in paragraph (5), by striking the period at the end and inserting a semicolon; and
- **(3)** by adding at the end the following:
  > - **(6)** $100,000,000 for fiscal year 2012; and
  > - **(7)** such sums as may be necessary for each subsequent fiscal year.
