# H.R. 296

> To amend title 10, United States Code, to revise the process by which a member of the Armed Forces is retired for disability and becomes eligible for retirement pay, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Armed Forces Disability Retirement Enhancement Act”.

Section 1074(c)(4) of title 10, United States Code, is amended—
