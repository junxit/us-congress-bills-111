# S. 4009

> To provide an alternate distribution of education jobs funds for Texas.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Fairness for Texas Schools Act of 2010”.

## § 2. Alternate distribution of funds to Texas

Section 101(11) of Public Law 111–226 is amended in subparagraph (C), by striking “Notwithstanding” and inserting “Except as provided under”.
