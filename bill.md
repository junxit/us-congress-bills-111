# H.R. 4834

> To amend section 493C of the Higher Education Act of 1965 to limit student loan payments to 10 percent of discretionary income, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

The Act may be cited as the “Student Debt Reduction Act”.

## § 2. Income-based repayment

Section 493C of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1098e) is amended—

- **(1)** in the matter preceding clause (i) of subsection (a)(3)(B), by striking “15 percent” and inserting “10 percent”; and
- **(2)** in the matter preceding clause (i) of subsection (b)(7)(B), by striking “25 years” and inserting “20 years”.
