# S. 2829

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to allow the cost of labor for building envelope improvements to be included for purposes of the nonbusiness energy property tax credit.

## § 1. Modification to nonbusiness energy property credit

- **(a)** *In general* Paragraph (1) of section 25C(c) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by adding at the end the following flush sentence:
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by this section shall apply to property placed in service after the date of the enactment of this Act.
