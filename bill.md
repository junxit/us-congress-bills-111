# S. 1453

> To amend Public Law 106–392 to maintain annual base funding for the Bureau of Reclamation for the Upper Colorado River and San Juan fish recovery programs through fiscal year 2023.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Bureau of Reclamation Fish Recovery Programs Reauthorization Act of 2009”.

## § 2. Reauthorization of base funding for fish recovery programs

Section 3(d)(2) of Public Law 106–392 (114 Stat. 1602) is amended in the fourth sentence by striking “2011” and inserting “2023”.
