# S. 2346

> To extend the reduction of duty on Mesotrione.

## § 1. Mesotrione

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.11.03 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to Mesotrione) is amended—
  - **(1)** in the general rate of duty column, by striking “6.11%” and inserting “5.1%”; and
  - **(2)** in the effective period column, by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
- **(c)** *Technical correction* Subchapter II of chapter 99 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended by striking heading 9902.25.80.
