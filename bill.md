# H.R. 2174

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 18 Main Street in Howland, Maine, as the “Clyde Hichborn Post Office”.

## § 1. Clyde Hichborn Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 18 Main Street in Howland, Maine, shall be known and designated as the “Clyde Hichborn Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Clyde Hichborn Post Office”.
