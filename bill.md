# H.R. 2098

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the look-through treatment of payments between related controlled foreign corporations.

## § 1. Extension of look-through treatment of payments between related controlled foreign corporations

Subparagraph (C) of section 954(c)(6) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “January 1, 2010” and inserting “January 1, 2015”.
