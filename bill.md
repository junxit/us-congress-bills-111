# H.R. 4257

> To amend the Energy Policy Act of 2005 relating to contracts for Federal purchases of renewable energy.

## § 1. Contracts for Federal purchases of renewable energy

Section 203 of the Energy Policy Act of 2005 (42 U.S.C. 15852) is amended—

- **(1)** by redesignating subsection (d) as subsection (e); and
- **(2)** by inserting after subsection (c) the following new subsection (d):
  > - **(d)** *Contracts for Renewable Energy* Notwithstanding section 501(b)(1)(B) of title 40, United States Code, a contract for the acquisition of renewable energy for the Federal Government may be made for a period of not more than 30 years.
