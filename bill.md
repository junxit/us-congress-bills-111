# S.J. RES. 24

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Department of Labor relating to financial disclosure and transparency by labor union management.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of Labor relating to financial disclosure and transparency by labor union management (published at 74 Fed. Reg. 52401 (October 13, 2009)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
