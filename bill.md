# S. 2630

> To extend temporarily the suspension of duty on plastic lamp-holder housings containing sockets.

## § 1. Plastic lamp-holder housings containing sockets

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.23.29 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to plastic lamp-holder housings containing sockets) is amended by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of enactment of this Act.
