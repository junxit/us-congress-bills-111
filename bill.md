# H.R. 2890

> To amend the Wild and Scenic Rivers Act to designate certain river segments in Oregon as wild or scenic rivers, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short Title

This Act may be cited as the “Lower Rogue Wild and Scenic River Expansion Act”.

- **(b)** *Withdrawal* Subject to valid rights, the Federal land within the boundaries of the river segments designated by subparagraph (B) of section 3(a)(5) of the Wild and Scenic Rivers Act (16 U.S.C. 1274(a)(5)), as added by subsection (a) of this section, is withdrawn from all forms of—
  - **(1)** entry, appropriation, or disposal under the public land laws;
  - **(2)** location, entry, and patent under the mining laws; and
  - **(3)** disposition under all laws pertaining to mineral and geothermal leasing or mineral materials.
