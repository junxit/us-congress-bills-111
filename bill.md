# H.R. 5706

> To designate the facility of the Government Printing Office located at 31451 East United Avenue in Pueblo, Colorado, as the “Frank Evans Government Printing Office Building”.

## § 1. Designation

The facility of the Government Printing Office located at 31451 East United Avenue in Pueblo, Colorado, shall be known and designated as the “Frank Evans Government Printing Office Building”.

## § 2. References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to the facility referred to in section 1 shall be considered to be a reference to the “Frank Evans Government Printing Office Building”.
