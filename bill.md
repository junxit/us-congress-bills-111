# S. RES. 448

> Reauthorizing the John Heinz Senate Fellowship Program.

## § 1. John Heinz Senate Fellowship Program

Senate Resolution 356, 102d Congress, agreed to October 7, 1992, is amended by striking section 5 and inserting the following:

> ## § 5. Funds
>
> There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out the provisions of this resolution $85,000 for each of fiscal years 2005 through 2014.
