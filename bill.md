# H.R. 4657

> To amend the Older Americans Act of 1965 to include information relating to the human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) in the disease prevention and health promotion services authorized by such Act.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Older Americans HIV Information and Prevention Act of 2010”.

## § 2. Amendments

Section 102(14) of the Older Americans Act of 1965 (42 U.S.C. 3002(14)) is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (B) by inserting “human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) infection,” after “density,”,
- **(2)** in subparagraph (D) by inserting “human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) infection,” after “diabetes,”, and
- **(3)** in subparagraph (J) by inserting “human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) infection,” after “diabetes,”.
