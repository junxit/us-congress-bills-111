# H. RES. 1166

> Directing the Clerk of the House of Representatives to establish and implement a process under which members of the public may view the proceedings of the House and the committees of the House online.

## § 1. Online Public Viewing of House and Committee Proceedings

- **(a)** *Establishment and Implementation of Process* The Clerk of the House of Representatives shall establish and implement a process under which members of the public may view the proceedings of the House and the proceedings of committees of the House online through official public Internet sites of the House.
- **(b)** *Regulations* The Clerk shall carry out this resolution in accordance with regulations promulgated by the Committee on House Administration.
