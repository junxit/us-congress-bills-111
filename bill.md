# H.R. 4882

> To direct the Secretary of the Army to conduct a study to determine the feasibility of carrying out a project to address the water resource development and management needs of the Soboba Band of Luiseño Indians Reservation, California.

## § 1. Soboba Band of Luiseño Indians Reservation, California

- **(a)** *In general* The Secretary of the Army shall conduct, at Federal expense, a study to determine the feasibility of carrying out a project to address the water resource development and management needs of the Soboba Band of Luiseño Indians Reservation, California.
- **(b)** *Authorization of appropriations* There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this section $1,700,000.
