# H.R. 2916

> To provide that no recreation grants made using funds from the Land and Water Conservation Fund may be used to acquire land or make improvements in State or local parks.

## § 1. Limitation on funding of National Heritage Areas

Notwithstanding any other law, no recreation grants made using funds from the Land and Water Conservation Fund may be used to acquire land or make improvements in State or local parks.
