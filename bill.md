# S. 1869

> To extend temporarily the suspension of duty on mixed xylidines.

## § 1. Mixed xylidines

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.22.36 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to mixed xylidines) is amended by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
