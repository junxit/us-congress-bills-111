# H.R. 4445

> To amend Public Law 95–232 to repeal a restriction on treating as Indian country certain lands held in trust for Indian pueblos in New Mexico.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Indian Pueblo Cultural Center Clarification Act”.

## § 2. Repeal of restriction on treating as Indian country certain lands held in trust for Indian pueblos in New Mexico

Public Law 95–232 is amended in the first section in subsection (b) by striking “However, such property shall not be “Indian country” as defined in section 1151 of title 18, United States Code.”.
