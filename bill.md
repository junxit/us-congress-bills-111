# H.R. 6330

> To make permanent the reduced individual income tax rate on dividends.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Preserving American Income on Dividends Act of 2010”.

## § 2. Reduced individual income tax rate on dividends made permanent

The Jobs and Growth Tax Relief Reconciliation Act of 2003 (relating to sunset of title) is amended by striking section 303.
