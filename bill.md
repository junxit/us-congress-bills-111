# H.R. 2053

> To designate the United States courthouse located at 525 Magoffin Avenue in El Paso, Texas, as the “Albert Armendariz, Sr., United States Courthouse”.

## § 1. Designation

The United States courthouse located at 525 Magoffin Avenue in El Paso, Texas, shall be known and designated as the “Albert Armendariz, Sr., United States Courthouse”.

## § 2. References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the United States courthouse referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Albert Armendariz, Sr., United States Courthouse”.
