# H.R. 3967

> To amend the National Great Black Americans Commemoration Act of 2004 to authorize appropriations through fiscal year 2015.

## § 1. Authorization of appropriations through fiscal year 2015

Section 3(c) of the National Great Black Americans Commemoration Act of 2004 is amended by striking “2009” and inserting “2015”.
