# S. 1883

> To extend and modify the temporary suspension of duty on iminodisuccinate.

## § 1. Iminodisuccinate

- **(a)** *In general* Subchapter II of chapter 99 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States is amended—
  - **(1)** in heading 9902.29.83 by striking the date in the effective period column, and inserting “12/31/2011”; and
  - **(2)** in heading 9902.38.10 by striking the date in the effective period column and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) apply to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
