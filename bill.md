# H.R. 2173

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 1009 Crystal Road in Island Falls, Maine, as the “Carl B. Smith Post Office”.

## § 1. Carl B. Smith Post Office

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 1009 Crystal Road in Island Falls, Maine, shall be known and designated as the “Carl B. Smith Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Carl B. Smith Post Office”.
