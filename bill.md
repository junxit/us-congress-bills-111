# H.R. 366

> To establish the national ocean exploration program and the national undersea research program within the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration, to direct the Administrator of the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration to establish and maintain an undersea research program, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Ocean Research and Exploration Enhancement Act of 2009”.
