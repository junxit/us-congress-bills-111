# H.R. 1634

> To amend title 23, United States Code, to extend the period during which States may allow low emission and energy-efficient vehicles to use high occupancy vehicle facilities.

## § 1. Use of HOV facilities by low emission and energy-efficient vehicles

Subparagraphs (A) and (B) of section 166(b)(5) are each amended by striking “September 30, 2009,” and inserting “July 1, 2012,”.
