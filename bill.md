# S. 2235

> To suspend temporarily the duty on certain educational toys or devices.

## § 1. Certain educational toys or devices

- **(a)** *In general* Subheading 9902.85.43 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to certain educational toys or devices) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “0.55%” and inserting “Free”; and
  - **(2)** in the effective period column, by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by subsection (a) applies to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
