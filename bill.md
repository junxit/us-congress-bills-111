# H.R. 5272

> To increase the maximum civil penalty for violations of Federal motor vehicle safety standards.

## § 1. Increase in maximum Civil penalties

Section 30165(a) of title 49, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “$5,000” both places it appears and inserting “$20,000”; and
- **(2)** by striking the last sentence of paragraphs (1) and (3), respectively.
