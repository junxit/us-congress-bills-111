# S. 3975

> To permanently extend the 2001 and 2003 tax relief provisions, and to permanently repeal the estate tax, and to provide permanent alternative minimum tax relief, and for other purposes.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Tax Relief Certainty Act”.
