# S. 2018

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on certain organic pigments and dyes.

## § 1. Certain organic pigments and dyes

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.32.07 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to certain organic pigments and dyes) is amended by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
