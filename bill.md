# S. 793

> To direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to establish a scholarship program for students seeking a degree or certificate in the areas of visual impairment and orientation and mobility.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Department of Veterans Affairs Vision Scholars Act of 2009”.
