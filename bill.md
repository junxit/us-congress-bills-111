# S. 3997

> To authorize appropriations for certain Native American programs.

## § 1. Authorization of appropriations for Native American programs

Section 816 of the Native American Programs Act of 1974 (42 U.S.C. 2992d) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a), by striking “fiscal years 1999, 2000, 2001, and 2002” and inserting “fiscal years 2011 through 2016”;
- **(2)** in subsection (c), by striking “fiscal years 1999, 2000, 2001, and 2002” and inserting “fiscal years 2011 through 2016”; and
- **(3)** in subsection (e), by striking “fiscal years 2008, 2009, 2010, 2011, and 2012” and inserting “fiscal years 2011 through 2016”.
