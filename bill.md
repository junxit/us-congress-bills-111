# H.R. 4411

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make permanent accelerated depreciation of natural gas distribution property and to clarify to which property such treatment applies.

## § 1. Accelerated depreciation of natural gas distribution facilities

- **(a)** *Accelerated depreciation made permanent* Clause (viii) of section 168(e)(3)(E) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by striking “, and which is placed in service before January 1, 2011”.
- **(b)** *Clarification of property to which treatment applies* Clause (viii) of section 168(e)(3)(E) of such Code is amended by striking “line” and inserting “facility”.
- **(c)** *Effective dates* The amendments made by this section shall apply to property placed in service after December 31, 2010.
