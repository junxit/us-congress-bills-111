# S.J. RES. 30

> Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the National Mediation Board relating to representation election procedures.

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the National Mediation Board relating to representation election procedures (published at 95 Fed. Reg. 26062 (May 11, 2010)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
