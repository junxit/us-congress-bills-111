# H.R. 1837

> To amend the Andean Trade Preference Act to add Paraguay to the list of countries that are eligible to be designated as beneficiary countries and ATPDEA beneficiary countries.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “U.S.-Paraguay Partnership Act of 2009”.

## § 2. Amendment to Andean Trade Preference Act

- **(a)** *Addition of Paraguay to list of eligible countries* Section 203(b)(1) of the Andean Trade Preference Act (19 U.S.C. 3202(b)(1)) is amended by inserting after “Colombia” the following:
  > -  Paraguay

- **(b)** *Termination of duty-free treatment* Section 208(a)(1) of such Act (19 U.S.C. 3206(a)(1)) is amended by striking “or Peru” and inserting “, Paraguay, or Peru”.
