# H.R. 4387

> To designate the Federal building located at 100 North Palafox Street in Pensacola, Florida, as the “Winston E. Arnow Federal Building”.

## § 1. Designation

The Federal building located at 100 North Palafox Street in Pensacola, Florida, shall be known and designated as the “Winston E. Arnow Federal Building”.

## § 2. References

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Winston E. Arnow Federal Building”.
