# H.R. 6490

> To amend the Soda Ash Royalty Reduction Act of 2006 to extend the reduced royalty rate for soda ash.

## § 1. Extension of reduction in royalty rate on soda ash

Section 102 of the Soda Ash Royalty Reduction Act of 2006 (Public Law 109–338; 30 U.S.C. 262 note) is amended by striking “5-year” and inserting “10-year”.
