# H.R. 3898

> To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the temporary increase in limitations on expensing of certain depreciable business assets.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Small Business Property Reinvestment Act of 2009”.

## § 2. Extension of temporary increase in limitations on expensing of certain depreciable business assets

- **(a)** *In general* Paragraph (7) of section 179(b) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “or 2009” and inserting “2009, or 2010”, and
  - **(2)** by striking “or 2009” in the heading thereof and inserting “2009, and 2010”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendments made by this section shall apply to taxable years beginning after December 31, 2009.
