# H.R. 3309

> To amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to postpone the increase in the minimum wage for 1 year.

## § 1. Postponement of wage increase

Section 6(a)(1)(C) of the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 (29 U.S.C. 206(a)(1)(C)) is amended by striking “beginning 24 months after that 60th day” and inserting “July 24, 2010”.
