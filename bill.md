# H.R. 6111

> To amend the Chesapeake and Ohio Canal Development Act to extend to the Chesapeake and Ohio Canal National Historical Park Commission.

## § 1. Chesapeake and Ohio Canal National Historical Park Commission

Section 6(g) of the Chesapeake and Ohio Canal Development Act (16 U.S.C. 410y–4(g)) is amended by striking “40” and inserting “50”.
