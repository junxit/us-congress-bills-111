# S. 3403

> To amend the Fish and Wildlife Improvement Act of 1978 to exempt subsistence users in the State of Alaska from the prohibition on taking.

## § 1. Exemption for subsistence users

Section 3(h)(2) of the Fish and Wildlife Improvement Act of 1978 (16 U.S.C. 712(1)) is amended by adding at the end: “A taking authorized under this section shall be exempt from the prohibition on taking under section 1 of the Migratory Bird Hunting and Conservation Stamp Act (16 U.S.C. 718a). ”
