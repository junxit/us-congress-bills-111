# H.R. 2544

> To require the intelligence community to use only methods of interrogation authorized by the United States Army Field Manual on Human Intelligence Collector Operations.

## § 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the “Legal Interrogation Procedures Act”.

## § 2. Limitation on use of interrogation techniques by the intelligence community

- **(a)** *Limitation* Title XI of the National Security Act of 1947 (50 U.S.C. 442 et seq.) is amended by adding at the end the following new section:
  > ## § 1103. Limitation on use of interrogation techniques by the intelligence community
  >
  > No person in the custody or under the effective control of an element of the intelligence community, or a contractor or subcontractor of such element, regardless of nationality or physical location, shall be subject to any treatment or technique of interrogation not authorized by the United States Army Field Manual on Human Intelligence Collector Operations.
