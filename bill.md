# H.R. 3348

> To amend the Digital Television Transition and Public Safety Act of 2005 to extend the interoperable emergency communications grant program through fiscal year 2012.

## § 1. Extension

Section 3006(a)(2) of the Digital Television Transition and Public Safety Act of 2005 is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “2010” and inserting “2012”; and
- **(2)** by inserting after the period at the end the following: “The Assistant Secretary may extend, on a case-by-case basis, the period of performance for any investment approved under the program for a period not to exceed 2 years.”
