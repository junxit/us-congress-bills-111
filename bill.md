# S. CON. RES. 17

> Authorizing the use of Emancipation Hall in the Capitol Visitor Center for the unveiling of a bust of Sojourner Truth.

## § 1. Use of Emancipation Hall for unveiling of Sojourner Truth bust

- **(a)** *Authorization* Emancipation Hall in the Capitol Visitor Center is authorized to be used for an event on April 28, 2009, to unveil a bust of Sojourner Truth.
- **(b)** *Preparations* Physical preparations for the conduct of the ceremony described in subsection (a) shall be carried out in accordance with such conditions as may be prescribed by the Architect of the Capitol.
