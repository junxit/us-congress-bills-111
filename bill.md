# H.R. 2047

> To authorize appropriations for Operation Stonegarden of the Department of Homeland Security.

## § 1. Authorization of appropriations for Operation Stonegarden

There is authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary of Homeland Security $500,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2010 through 2014 to carry out Operation Stonegarden.
