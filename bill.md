# S. 2197

> To extend the temporary suspension of duty on a certain ion exchange resin.

## § 1. A certain ion exchange resin

- **(a)** *In general* Heading 9902.02.33 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (relating to a certain ion exchange resin) is amended by striking “12/31/2009” and inserting “12/31/2011”.
- **(b)** *Effective date* The amendment made by subsection (a) applies with respect to goods entered, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the 15th day after the date of the enactment of this Act.
