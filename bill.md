# H.R. 5278

> To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 405 West Second Street in Dixon, Illinois, as the “President Ronald W. Reagan Post Office Building”.

## § 1. President Ronald W. Reagan Post Office Building

- **(a)** *Designation* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 405 West Second Street in Dixon, Illinois, shall be known and designated as the “President Ronald W. Reagan Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “President Ronald W. Reagan Post Office Building”.
