# S. 1372

> To provide a vehicle maintenance building to house the Smithsonian Institution's Vehicle Maintenance Branch at the Suitland Collections Center in Suitland, Maryland.

## § 1. Vehicle maintenance building

The Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution is authorized to plan, design, and construct a vehicle maintenance building at its Vehicle Maintenance Branch in Suitland, Maryland, to house, maintain, and repair Smithsonian vehicles and transportation equipment.

## § 2. Authorization of appropriations

There are authorized to be appropriated $4,000,000 for fiscal year 2010 for the purposes described in section 1.
